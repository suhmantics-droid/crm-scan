# Customer service SOP

Dated 2026-08-31. Service is a conversion asset here: responsive sellers turn breakage into 5★ reviews (documented pattern), and Star Seller status (fast replies) feeds search placement.

## Standards
- First reply within 24h (aim same-day). Etsy app notifications on.
- Tone: plain, warm, competent. No corporate apology templates.
- Every support answer that repeats gets promoted into the product's FAQ/guide within a week (the documented complaint "instructions should be updated to include what customer service told them" is a free product-improvement engine).

## Canned frameworks (draft with AI, send by human)
1. **"It's not working"**: thank → ask for format (Sheets/Excel) + screenshot → known-issues check → fix or escalate to same-day file fix → follow up next day.
2. **"How do I...?"**: answer + timestamped video link to the exact moment.
3. **Refund request**: fix-first offer; if they still want out, refund without friction (digital margin makes goodwill cheap; a fight costs a 1★).
4. **"Can you make a version for X?"**: log in the vertical-request register (this is free product research); if ≥3 requests, it enters the roadmap.
5. **Review with a problem in it**: public reply: acknowledge, state the fix shipped, invite them to re-download. Future buyers read this.

## Update delivery
- Tax-year updates: replace listing files each March + Etsy message to past buyers ("free updated 2027/28 file is in your downloads").
- The update message may include the optional email-list link for early access (accurate claims only, no spam).

## Boundaries
- No tax advice, ever: "I can show you what the sheet does; for your tax position please check gov.uk or an accountant" (canned).
- No custom builds below £50; personalisation add-on covers small asks.
- Abusive buyers: refund, block, move on.

## Weekly metrics (Friday sweep)
Open messages · avg response time · issues by product (top recurring = next fix) · review rate + rating trend · requests register additions.
