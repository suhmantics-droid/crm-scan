# Shopify expansion strategy

Dated 2026-08-31. Principle from the brief honoured: do not recommend Shopify prematurely, and keep aufentic.suhmantics.com out of this entirely.

## Trigger conditions (do NOT build before these)

Open the Shopify property only when ALL of:
1. Etsy shop ≥ £750/mo for 2 consecutive months (proves product-market fit worth porting).
2. ≥ 300 total orders (enough buyers to seed email/audience).
3. At least one product family with repeat/bundle behaviour (data shows what premium buyers want).

Expected timing if BASE case holds: month 6-9. Until then, 100% of effort compounds better inside Etsy's existing traffic.

## What goes where

| Stays Etsy-exclusive | Graduates to Shopify |
|---|---|
| Entry products (£4.99-9.99): they are customer acquisition | Mega bundles and "complete office" packs (£49-99): Etsy fee % + price ceiling perceptions cap these |
| Single trade kits | "Pro" versions with onboarding call/setup service (£99+): needs trust a marketplace can't carry |
| Seasonal editions (search-entry value) | The update subscription (see below) |
| Anything still validating | Email-driven launches (new tax year editions to the list) |

## The Shopify property

- **Separate brand site** (the Etsy shop's name, its own .co.uk bought day 1), NOT suhmantics.com and NOT aufentic: a UK money-tools brand needs its own trust surface, and the asset stays cleanly sellable. suhmantics.com can hold a portfolio/about page linking to it at most.
- Stack: Shopify Basic + Digital Downloads app (or SendOwl). Break-even vs Etsy fees needs only ~£120-150/mo of direct sales.

## Email capture (starts BEFORE Shopify)

- Month 1: every download's quick-start PDF and video description offers "free tax-year updates + the 5-minute Friday money routine" via a signup link (allowed: it is product support/updates, not spam; keep claims accurate). Landing page: free Carrd/ConvertKit page until Shopify exists.
- Target: 20-30% of buyers onto the list. 300 orders → ~75-90 subscribers → enough for a real launch by tax-year refresh (March).

## Subscriptions (only where honest)

- Candidate: "Tax Year Club" £12-19/yr: guaranteed day-one new-year editions of everything owned, plus quarterly MTD-habit checklists. Only launch if update demand proves out (support inbox + email replies will tell us). Do not force subscription on a category whose buyers chose one-time purchase to escape subscriptions: the club must be obviously optional and additive.

## Customer ownership and cross-selling

- Etsy owns Etsy buyers (no off-platform redirection in listings beyond permitted update/support flows). The email list is the bridge asset.
- Cross-selling on Shopify: family grids + "complete your office" logic mirroring the Etsy bundle map.
- Higher-ticket path (Shopify-only): "set up for you" personalisation (£49-99), team/multi-user licences for small firms (bookkeepers buying for clients: a real observed buyer type in this category).
