Dated 2026-08-31. Format per brief: THESIS / CUSTOMER / PROBLEM / PRODUCT / DEMAND / COMPETITION / PRICE / MARGIN / WHY WE WIN / RISKS / CATALOGUE / £1K PATH / £5K PATH. Margins are net of the UK fee stack in research/etsy-policy.md (≈14-18% take at these prices, ~84-86% net).

## 1. UK Sole Trader Bookkeeping + Self Assessment Tracker
- **THESIS**: The proven premium Etsy digital category (bookkeeping spreadsheets) intersected with a UK regulatory deadline nobody on the platform owns yet.
- **CUSTOMER**: UK sole traders and self-employed, especially the 864k pulled into MTD from Apr 2026 and the £30k wave behind them (Apr 2027).
- **PROBLEM**: Records in a shoebox; January dread; new legal duty to keep digital records; subscription software resented.
- **PRODUCT**: Google Sheets + Excel workbook: income/expense ledgers with HMRC-aligned categories, bank-statement-friendly entry, monthly/annual dashboards, self-assessment summary tab with box mapping, tax set-aside estimator, mileage log; quick-start PDF + 10-min walkthrough video; free tax-year updates.
- **DEMAND**: VERIFIED comparables (see competitors.csv); category leader in adjacent vertical: 3.1k reviews. UK volumes: WEEK-1 CHECK.
- **COMPETITION**: Individually decent listings, no branded family, common failure modes documented (fragile formulas, US defaults, no support).
- **PRICE**: £16.99 (bundle £29.99+). **MARGIN**: ~84% net after fees.
- **WHY WE WIN**: Engineering QA + process design + support promise + Sheets/Excel parity + annual updates: every differentiator maps to a documented complaint.
- **RISKS**: seasonality trough (spring/summer); a strong incumbent could copy the UK-MTD positioning; accuracy errors would be reputation-fatal (mitigated by qa-sop.md).
- **£1K PATH**: this product + its 4 satellites at ~75 orders/mo blended. **£5K PATH**: the full 150-listing catalogue + email + off-Etsy (see finance/).

## 3. Simple CRM + Follow-up Engine
- **THESIS**: Every Etsy "CRM template" is a formatted table; a product that encodes actual pipeline discipline (stages with exit criteria, next-action-date engine, today-view, follow-up cadence, win-rate maths) has no visible competitor at any price.
- **CUSTOMER**: freelancers, consultants, coaches, recruiters, tradespeople who quote: anyone with 10-200 leads and no CRM.
- **PROBLEM**: "leads slip through because I follow up from memory."
- **DEMAND**: VERIFIED listings exist across verticals; depth gap observed. Volume: MEDIUM confidence, WEEK-1 CHECK.
- **PRICE**: £14.99. **MARGIN**: ~84%.
- **WHY WE WIN**: 15 years of the seller's actual domain. The listing copy writes itself and no printable-shop competitor can fake it.
- **RISKS**: "CRM" may be browse-intent on Etsy; mitigation: problem-term SEO ("lead tracker", "follow up tracker").
- **CATALOGUE**: vertical clones (recruiter, estate agent, salon, wedding vendor) at 1 day each.

## 4. Cleaning Business Kit
- **THESIS**: "Boring money" per-trade operating kits: dense proven demand (cleaning market pages), weak incumbents, infinite verticalization.
- **PRODUCT**: quote calculator (hourly/room/sqm with margin floor) + job & payment tracker + money ledger + price list + invoice, one workbook.
- **PRICE**: £12.99. **WHY WE WIN**: incumbents sell documents; we sell an operating system for the business.
- **RISKS**: demand per individual trade is modest; the model works as a family, not a single listing.
