# Top 3 concepts, developed

Dated 2026-08-31. Three shop-level concepts (not single products). Each is a coherent brand a buyer could describe in one sentence.

---

## Concept A: "UK Small Business Money" (spreadsheet-first finance & ops shop)

- **BRAND**: working name SortedSheets (options in positioning.md). Voice: plain-English, HMRC-literate, quietly confident. Anti-hype.
- **CUSTOMER**: UK one-person businesses: sole traders, freelancers, service trades, landlords, resellers.
- **POSITIONING**: "Money admin for UK small businesses, done properly. £ not $, HMRC categories not IRS, video guides, both formats, free tax-year updates, real support."
- **PRODUCT RANGE (launch 10)**: Sole Trader Bookkeeping+Tax (flagship £16.99), Freelancer edition (£14.99), Tax Estimator (£9.99), Mileage Log (£4.99), Invoice+Chase Tracker (£9.99), Cleaning Kit (£12.99), Dog Groomer Kit (£12.99), Salon Kit (£12.99), Trades Kit (£12.99), CRM+Follow-up (£14.99). Bundles: "Self-Employed Starter" (flagship+estimator+mileage £24.99), "Run Your Trade" (kit+invoice+CRM £29.99).
- **PRICING LOGIC**: entry £4.99-8.99 funnels; core £12.99-16.99; bundles £24.99-34.99 lift AOV toward £15-18.
- **SHOP STRUCTURE**: sections = By trade / Tax & bookkeeping / Client & sales tools / Bundles.
- **SEO**: own the UK-qualified long tail first ("bookkeeping spreadsheet uk hmrc", "self assessment spreadsheet", per-trade terms), then climb to generic heads.
- **CONTENT**: Pinterest (tax-tip + template pins), TikTok/Shorts ("UK tax facts for self-employed" + screen demos), all via Share & Save links.
- **BUNDLES/UPSELLS**: every listing cross-links its bundle; post-purchase message offers companion at small discount (within Etsy rules).
- **ROADMAP**: Q1: launch 10 + iterate on data; Q2: landlord + reseller + 6 trade verticals; Q3: tax-year 2027/28 editions + email capture; Q4: Shopify graduation of winners.
- **FINANCIAL MODEL**: see finance/. Base case: £1k/mo by month 5-7.

## Concept B: "Pipeline" (sales tools for people who sell without a CRM)

- **BRAND**: sales-method-led shop; voice of a sales director, not a designer.
- **CUSTOMER**: freelancers/consultants/recruiters/agencies/trades who quote.
- **RANGE**: CRM+Follow-up Engine, pipeline dashboards, commission calculators, outreach trackers, quote-conversion tracker, per-vertical editions.
- **WHY IT COULD WIN**: deepest differentiation moat of any concept; the only shop where the seller's CV is the moat.
- **WHY IT IS NOT #1**: raw Etsy demand signals for sales tools are visibly thinner than money/tax; buyer urgency is not calendar-driven; £1k/mo purely on this line needs stronger volumes than the evidence supports. VERDICT: build as the second line inside Concept A's shop, spin out if it outperforms.

## Concept C: "The Trade Office" (per-trade business-in-a-box kits)

- **BRAND**: one kit per trade, aggressively verticalized listings.
- **CUSTOMER**: cleaners, gardeners, groomers, decorators, mobile beauty, window cleaners.
- **RANGE**: 10-15 trade kits + shared satellites (mileage, invoices, pricing calculators).
- **WHY IT COULD WIN**: weakest incumbents, warmest buyer identity match, huge catalogue math.
- **WHY IT IS NOT #1**: each vertical's individual demand is modest and UNVERIFIED at volume; the concept lacks the January/April demand spikes that de-risk year one. VERDICT: it is Concept A's growth engine rather than a standalone shop.

## Decision

**Concept A absorbs B and C as product lines.** One shop, one buyer identity (UK one-person business), three lines: money/tax (proven demand + catalyst), trade kits (catalogue engine), sales tools (moat). This maximizes expected value while letting shop data reallocate effort quarterly. Full rationale and single-winner spec in winner.md.
