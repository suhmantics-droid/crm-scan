# Positioning and branding

Dated 2026-08-31.

## Shop name options

Requirements: reads as a product brand (not a printables shop), UK-friendly, no trademark collisions with accounting SaaS (avoid anything near QuickBooks/Xero/FreeAgent/Sage), works off-Etsy later. Check availability (Etsy, .co.uk, Companies House, IPO trademark search) before committing: 30-day plan, day 1.

1. **SortedSheets** (recommended): "sorted" is UK vernacular for exactly the promise; descriptive of format; room for "Sorted" sub-brands (SortedBooks, SortedClients).
2. **TallyKit**: friendly, ledger heritage word, kit-based catalogue fits.
3. **TheSoloBooks**: names the customer (solo business) and the job (books); slightly narrower fit for the CRM line.

## One-line positioning

"Money and client admin for UK one-person businesses: spreadsheets done properly."

## Tagline options
- "Your business, sorted."
- "£ not $. HMRC not IRS. Sorted."
- "Buy once. Use every year."

## Shop description (draft)

Spreadsheets and business tools built for UK sole traders, freelancers and small service businesses. Everything is UK-first: pounds, DD/MM dates, HMRC-aligned categories and the UK tax year. Every product comes in native Google Sheets AND Excel versions, with a quick-start guide, a video walkthrough, locked formulas you can't break, free tax-year updates, and a real person answering messages within 24 hours.

## About section (draft)

Built by a UK B2B operator with a background in sales, CRM systems and business process design. These aren't decorated tables: they're the systems I'd run a business on, engineered and tested like software, then made simple enough to use in five minutes a week. (Human-checked claim: keep it true and modest; no income claims.)

## Visual identity

- **Direction**: "engineered, not crafted". Clean fintech-adjacent look; deliberately unlike the pastel-planner aesthetic, so thumbnails stand out in category search results.
- **Colour**: deep green (money/HMRC-adjacent) + off-white + one warm accent (amber) for CTAs; consistent across every thumbnail.
- **Thumbnail system** (see listings/image-briefs.md): laptop/phone mockup of the dashboard + 3-5 word benefit headline + UK badge + edition year chip. Identical layout across the catalogue so the shop reads as a product family in search results.
- **Type**: one clean sans (e.g. Inter/Archivo) everywhere; no script fonts.

## Ownership decision (per brief section 30)

**B, becoming C: a separate Etsy brand, built to graduate to its own Shopify property later.** Not Suhmantics, because:
- Suhmantics/aufentic is a dropshipping context; attaching a trust-first UK finance brand to it helps neither (explicitly keeping aufentic separate per the operator's instruction).
- A standalone brand is sellable later as a clean asset (shop + files + email list).
- If Suhmantics becomes a portfolio holding entity, the shop can sit under it legally without sharing customer-facing identity.

Recommendation: register the Etsy shop under the new name; buy the matching .co.uk on day 1 (~£10; the one optional cost); park it until Shopify phase (strategy/shopify-strategy.md).
