# Catalogue strategy and the catalogue effect

Dated 2026-08-31.

## What the evidence says about catalogue size

Honest state of the data: no rigorous public dataset ties listing count to sales causally. What exists:
- Top digital shops are repeatedly described as running 50-200 optimised listings (REPORTED, aggregator).
- Etsy's own seller guidance and every practitioner source agree each listing is an additional search entry; variety widens keyword coverage (REPORTED).
- Case-study shops (e.g. the $168k/yr digital store) attribute growth to niched catalogue depth (REPORTED, unaudited).
- Community anecdotes cut both ways: bulk-listing junk does nothing; each listing must clear the quality bar. ANECDOTAL.

**Verdict: MORE LISTINGS = MORE OPPORTUNITY, conditional on per-listing quality.** Breadth is how a no-audience shop buys lottery tickets in search; quality is what converts the ticket. The strategy is therefore: engineered quality floor (SOPs) + steady breadth (2-3 listings/week) + quarterly pruning.

## Catalogue economics model (LOW / BASE / HIGH)

Assumptions labelled: orders/listing/month for digital shops has no published average (UNKNOWN); we model a skewed distribution consistent with practitioner reports: most listings ~0-0.5 orders/mo, winners carry multiples. AOV £15 blended (our price ladder). Net per order ≈ £12.75 (fees per etsy-policy.md, mixed offsite-ads incidence). MODELLED RESULTS, not forecasts.

Seasonality multiplier on the money/tax line: treat Sept-Jan as 1.5-2.5x the trough months (INFERRED from category seasonality; validate with year-1 data).

Reading it honestly: BASE at 100 listings is ~£640/mo; £1k/mo needs either HIGH-performing listings (a few winners carrying the shop, which is how the category actually behaves) or >150 listings or the seasonal peak. This is why the plan front-loads tax season and review velocity.

## Rules

1. **Never ship below the quality floor** (both formats, locked cells, guide, video, demo data). One bad listing's reviews poison the shop's conversion everywhere.
2. **Clone engines, not effort**: a new listing should cost ~1 day (vertical skin) not 3 (new engine).
3. **Prune quarterly**: after 90 days, a listing with <1% conversion and <30 views/mo gets re-SEO'd once; if still dead in 60 more days, deactivate (its £0.15 renewal is trivial but its conversion drag is not).
4. **Duplicate-intent listings are allowed but distinct**: "cleaning business bookkeeping" and "cleaner income tracker" can be separate listings with different lead keywords and thumbnails; never identical listings (policy risk).
5. **Editions are new SKUs**: "2027/28" editions get new listings (fresh search entries + recency) while past buyers get free file updates (retention promise).
