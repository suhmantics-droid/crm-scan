# Route B: the independent modular A5 planner line

Decision recorded 2026-09-03: Route B chosen (independent original line; no Agende partnership assumed). This file is the operating spec. Prototype built same day: `../prototypes/leafline-a5-planner-prototype.pdf` (generator: `generate_a5_planner.py`).

## Brand

Working name **Leafline** ("loose-leaf" + a clean line; the wordmark is LEAFLINE followed by a teal full stop). Alternates if name checks fail: Quireworks, Southpaw Paper Co (if the left-handed wedge becomes the whole brand). Day-1 checks as usual: Etsy, .co.uk, IPO trademark, Companies House. Avoid anything echoing Agende, Filofax, or other planner marks.

Positioning: **"The modular A5 planner: undated, hyperlinked, and made for both hands."**

Voice and look: quiet, engineered, warm-paper minimalism. Ink #23262B on paper #FBF9F4, one deep teal accent #31555A. Deliberately NOT burgundy/gold (Agende's world) and not pastel-planner.

## Differentiators (each mapped to evidence)

1. **Native left-handed mirrored editions.** Etsy has dedicated market pages for [left handed planner](https://www.etsy.com/market/left_handed_planner), [planners for left handed](https://www.etsy.com/market/planners_for_left_handed) and even [left handed teacher planner](https://www.etsy.com/market/left_handed_teacher_planner): real search demand, and no visible brand leads with it. VERIFIED demand exists / volume UNKNOWN (Marketplace Insights, day 2).
2. **Modular section packs**, digital and printable: the razor/blade structure (shell once, refills forever).
3. **Undated**: no wasted stock, evergreen listings, no annual obsolescence except optional dated editions.
4. **Print-and-file A5 refills** for any ring/disc binder: the punch-guide page keeps a 13mm inner margin clear rather than claiming any binder standard.
5. **Engineering QA** carried over from the main line (working links tested, print-scale tested, both-hands versions of every writing page).

## IP guardrails (restated, non-negotiable)

Nothing of Agende's: no layouts copied, no names, no "method" content, no references to the brand in listings or tags. The modular-insert concept and left-hand mirroring are unprotectable ideas; their expression of them is off limits. All Leafline layouts are original (the prototype was designed from scratch in code). Fonts for production must carry embedding/commercial licences (prototype uses base Helvetica; budget one licensed family, logged in the licence register).

## Season and sequencing

- Planner demand peaks **Nov-Jan** (new year) with a secondary **September** back-to-term bump. Target: SKUs 1-4 listed by **late October 2026**, dated 2027 edition first week of November.
- **Route B does not displace SortedSheets.** It is a separate Etsy shop (different buyer, different voice) on a ≤20% effort budget until data argues otherwise. SortedSheets' tax-season launch keeps priority; Leafline's own season conveniently starts a few weeks later.
- Shared infrastructure: the same production pipeline, QA SOP, listing SOP, image system (re-skinned), and Share & Save discipline.

## Build cost (measured, not guessed)

The 13-page hyperlinked shell prototype took one focused session including two visual QA passes: structure, linked tab rail, six section types, and the mirrored lefty page, all generated from one Python file, meaning every future variant (dated, teacher, student) is an edit + regenerate, not a redesign. Realistic cost to sale-ready SKU: **1-2 days** including licensed font swap, cover art, on-device GoodNotes test, listing images and video. This confirms the £-zero, time-cheap economics assumed in the research.
