# THE WINNER

Dated 2026-08-31. Per brief section 27: one choice, no "it depends".

## WINNER

**A UK small-business money & operations spreadsheet shop, spearheaded by the UK Sole Trader Bookkeeping + Self Assessment Tracker (Google Sheets + Excel), expanding into per-trade business kits and a sales/CRM line.** Working brand: SortedSheets.

## WHY

1. **Proven premium category.** Bookkeeping spreadsheets are the demonstrated big winner of "boring" Etsy digital: a comparable listing carries 3.1k reviews at 4.8★ (VERIFIED), an adjacent premium brand (Paper and Spark) sells the same buyer at US$25-70 with "lifetime updates" as the hook.
2. **A regulatory tailwind with dates on it.** MTD for Income Tax: digital records + quarterly updates mandatory from 6 Apr 2026 (864,000 sole traders/landlords), £30k threshold Apr 2027, £20k planned 2028 (VERIFIED). Nobody visible on Etsy owns "MTD-ready records" positioning yet. Windows like this close.
3. **The market's documented failures are our exact strengths.** Broken formulas, no instructions, US defaults, no support, single-format files: every recurring complaint is an engineering/process failure, and the seller's edge is engineering, process, and B2B credibility, not graphic design.
4. **Catalogue mathematics.** One QA'd spreadsheet engine skins into dozens of vertical listings (trade kits, landlord, reseller, driver), each a new search entry, each ~1 day of work via the production system in operations/.
5. **Calendar-driven demand.** Sept-Jan self-assessment season, April new tax year: two guaranteed annual spikes, plus evergreen trades demand between them. Launching September 2026 rides straight into the strong season.
6. **Second-best (sales/CRM tools) folds in as a line, not a loss.** The winner absorbs the runner-up's moat.

Verdict: **POSSIBLE BUT DIFFICULT** inside 12 months; realistic at month 12-18 if month-6 base case lands. Full math in finance/£5k-model.md.

## BIGGEST RISK
**Discovery failure in months 1-3** (the digital-shop cold start): mitigated by launching into tax season, entry-price products for review velocity, Pinterest from day 1, and 20 listings by day 30. Second risk: an accuracy error in a tax-adjacent product harming trust: mitigated by qa-sop.md and "records tool, not advice" framing everywhere.

## SECOND-BEST OPTION
The standalone **sales/CRM tools shop** (Concept B in top-3.md): deepest moat, weaker demand evidence and no calendar catalyst. It ships as line 3 of the winner instead of dying as a runner-up.
