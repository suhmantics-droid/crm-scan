# Customer problems: the language of the buyers

Dated 2026-08-31. Real problem language gathered from forums, review excerpts surfaced in search, seller-community threads and category research. Where a phrase is quoted it was surfaced by a cited source; paraphrases are marked. Reddit was used for language and problems only, not market sizing (per brief). Labels: PATTERN (3+ independent signals), ANECDOTAL (single source).

## 1. UK sole traders and freelancers: money admin

- PATTERN: tax-season dread and shoebox record keeping. The entire UK self-assessment template market exists because records live in bank statements and carrier bags until January. Product copy from established listings mirrors the anxiety: layouts "mirror HMRC Self Assessment format" with "box numbers noted" ([Etsy listing 4373164546](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4373164546/bookkeeping-tax-spreadsheet-for-sole), [businessaccountingbasics.co.uk](https://www.businessaccountingbasics.co.uk/self-employed-bookkeeping/)).
- PATTERN: subscription resentment. Documented buyer motivation for templates over software: "instead of connecting your bank and paying a yearly subscription for an app that might shut down tomorrow, you get Google Sheets templates you own forever... keeping your data in your own Google account" ([source](https://ddg.wcroc.umn.edu/home-accounting-software-without-subscription)); subscription fatigue and rising-cost fear are recurring ([techradar.com](https://techradar.com/pro/software-services/subscription-vs-one-time-purchase-software-we-analyzed-the-true-cost-for-smbs)).
- NEW, dated: MTD confusion. 864,000 people pulled into quarterly digital reporting from April 2026; sole trader + landlord = two updates per quarter ([bytestart.co.uk](https://www.bytestart.co.uk/news-insights/864000-sole-traders-and-landlords-face-new-mtd-reporting-rules-from-april-2026/), [gocardless.com](https://gocardless.com/blog/mtd-itsa-sole-traders-landlords-2026-guide)). The problem phrasing to serve: "what records do I actually need to keep", "do I need software now", "my accountant wants everything categorised".
- Problem statements the products answer:
  - "I don't know which expenses I can claim." (HMRC category mapping)
  - "I just need something my accountant can use." (accountant-ready export tab)
  - "I'm scared I'll owe more than I saved." (running tax-set-aside calculator)

## 2. Small service businesses (cleaners, trades, salons, groomers)

- PATTERN: no back office. The per-trade Etsy market pages (cleaning invoices, cleaning quote sheets, estimate templates: [market pages VERIFIED](https://www.etsy.com/market/cleaning_business_estimate_template)) exist because these businesses run on paper and WhatsApp.
- Problem statements:
  - "I quote off the top of my head and I think I'm undercharging." (quote calculator with margin floor)
  - "I forget who owes me." (job + payment tracker)
  - "I lose track of regulars' details." (client cards / lightweight CRM)
- ANECDOTAL: buyers of these templates praise time saved and looking professional to clients; the products succeed as identity ("a real business now") as much as function.

## 3. UK landlords

- PATTERN: tax complexity outgrew the spreadsheet they built themselves. Competitor products advertise Section 24 handling, gross/net yield, HMRC expense categories, certificate tracking ([Etsy listing 4481759113](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4481759113/landlord-property-tracker-202627-uk)); records must be kept 5 years 10 months for property income ([landlordstax.co.uk](https://www.landlordstax.co.uk/services/your-records/)).
- Problem statements: "one place for rent, expenses, and what the taxman needs"; "which certificates expire when"; "is this property actually making money".

## 4. Solo sales operators (freelancers, recruiters, coaches, small agencies)

- PATTERN: CRM SaaS is overkill; self-built spreadsheets decay. The verticalized lead-tracker listings (for coaches, for real estate: [Etsy listing 1735414707](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1735414707/lead-tracker-spreadsheet-for-coaches-crm)) show the demand shape: people search for a tracker for *their* business, not "CRM".
- Problem statements: "leads slip through because I follow up from memory"; "I don't need Salesforce, I need a list that tells me who to chase today"; "I want to see my pipeline without paying monthly".
- Gap observed: existing products are formatted tables. Almost none embed actual sales method (stage definitions, next-action discipline, follow-up cadence, win-rate maths). That is our differentiator.

## 6. What buyers of spreadsheet/template products complain about (the quality gap)

From review excerpts and category research (see review-mining.md for the full set):

- PATTERN: **broken or fragile formulas** and unprotected cells: "cells that have formulas should be locked... I had to reload the spreadsheet a second time and start from scratch" (buyer review quoted via [craftybase.com](https://craftybase.com/blog/etsy-spreadsheets) research pass).
- PATTERN: **instructions unclear or missing**; buyers ask customer service for what should be in the guide.
- PATTERN: **US-centric assumptions**: $ signs, US tax categories, MM/DD dates, sales-tax logic that means nothing to a UK buyer.
- PATTERN: **format confusion**: Excel file opened in Google Sheets (or vice versa) breaks; buyers do not distinguish the formats until it fails.
- PATTERN: sellers who answer fast and fix files convert complaints into 5★ reviews ("seller promptly answered and corrected the error").
