## The segment thesis

The unifying customer is the **UK one-person business** (sole trader, freelancer, service trade, landlord-as-business, reseller). One buyer identity, one shop voice, dozens of vertical products. They are on Etsy already (proven listings), they buy £8-30 tools with money attached to a deadline, and every complaint pattern in the category is a process failure we can out-engineer. Consumer segments are kept only as later cross-sells (budget sheet for the sole trader who liked the business one).
