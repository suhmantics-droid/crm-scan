## 3. Why a digital version makes commercial sense

1. **The architecture is already digital-product architecture.** A binder + swappable packs = a hub product + SKU packs. Their catalogue translates one-to-one (table below) with zero fulfilment.
2. **The £279 anchor.** Against a £279 physical set, a £29-49 "digital edition" reads cheap while sitting 3-5x above typical Etsy digital-planner pricing. Premium digital planners survive only with a premium anchor; Agende has one.
3. **The pre-order gap.** Their structural weakness is wait time. "Start today with the digital edition while your leather is made" converts waiters, reduces pre-order remorse, and costs nothing per unit.
4. **Refills are the recurring revenue.** Ring-planner economics are razor/blade: printable and digital pack refills (dated years especially) repeat annually at ~100% margin.
5. **The left-hand mirrored layout is a genuinely underserved wedge.** No visible printable-insert seller leads with left-handed editions; Agende's own feature signals the demand.

## 5. The IP line (non-negotiable)

Two routes exist and only one of them may use anything of theirs:

- **ROUTE A: WITH Agende.** Official digital line under their brand: their designs, their name, "Isobel's Method" content, her audience as distribution. Structure: we build and operate (production system, Etsy/Shopify ops, GTM: exactly the operator's stack) on a revenue share; digital packs upsold on every physical order and to the waitlist. This is the high-EV route because **distribution is the scarce asset and she has it**: a creator audience turns "saturated category" maths upside down.
- **ROUTE B: INDEPENDENT.** An original modular A5 planner system: our own layouts, names, cover designs, and method content. The modular-insert *concept* and left-hand mirroring are not protectable ideas; their specific designs, copy, branding, and workbook content are. Nothing of Agende's gets copied, referenced, or traded on. Route B's honest wedge is not "another digital planner" (scored 44-48/100, SATURATED, in our top-50): it is **printable A5 ring-binder refills + left-handed editions + premium digital planner shell**, sold under a separate lifestyle sub-brand (NOT SortedSheets: different buyer, different voice), validated by Cloth & Paper's hybrid model.

Etsy policy notes: manifesting journals are a large, allowed category; keep copy free of outcome promises (policy claims risk). AI disclosure rules as per etsy-policy.md. Undated versions extend product life; dated versions drive annual repurchase.
