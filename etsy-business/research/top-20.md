## Survivors (in final order after second pass)

### 1. UK Sole Trader Bookkeeping + Self Assessment Tracker — HOLDS #1
- Demand: VERIFIED comparable listings (multiple, some ranking for 2+ years); MTD wave adds 864k mandated record-keepers from Apr 2026, £30k threshold Apr 2027 adds more. Seasonal spike Jan (self assessment).
- Competition: existing UK listings are individually decent but the field lacks a brand: no visible shop owns "UK sole trader money" with a full product family, Sheets+Excel parity, video onboarding and update policy. WEEK-1 CHECK: review counts of top 5 UK listings.
- Pricing: £14.99-19.99 sits under accountant-grade (US$25-70 off-Etsy) and above commodity. Chosen: £16.99.
- Policy/IP: LOW/LOW. Copy rules (no advice, no "MTD compliant filing") documented in etsy-policy.md.
- Catalogue: exceptional: trade verticals x tax-year editions x add-ons.
- Shopify: strong (brand can graduate; updates delivered off-platform later).
