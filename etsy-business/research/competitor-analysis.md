# Competitor analysis: patterns, weaknesses, and the openings

Dated 2026-08-31. Built from competitors.csv. Honest limitation: review counts and prices for most individual listings were not retrievable from this environment (etsy.com blocked to direct fetch); the analysis leans on what IS visible: which products exist, how they position, what features they advertise, and the few hard numbers that surfaced (3.1k reviews on the category leader; 4,741 favourites on another; 1,324 monthly searches for "google sheets budget"). Week-1 task: re-run this file's tables with live review counts from the Etsy app.

## What exists and sells (demand patterns)

1. **The bookkeeping spreadsheet is the proven premium winner.** The Etsy-seller vertical alone supports a 3.1k-review listing plus an off-Etsy premium brand (Paper and Spark) whose core pitch is accountant credibility and lifetime updates. Multi-thousand-review digital listings at £8-25 exist in this family; nothing in the CRM or trades families showed comparable visible traction.
2. **Verticalization is the growth move competitors already validate.** Lead trackers "for coaches"; bookkeeping "for Etsy sellers"; landlord trackers by portfolio size (1-6, 10, 15 properties). Buyers search for their situation, not the generic tool.
3. **UK-specific tax products exist and persist.** Listings mirroring HMRC Self Assessment boxes, some ranking for years (a 2023-branded listing still surfaces). Persistence implies sales; exact volumes UNKNOWN.
4. **The landlord niche already has a sophisticated leader** (758 formulas, Section 24 logic, 2026/27 tax-year edition). Beatable on support/instructions/Sheets-parity, but not an easy lead.
5. **Trades paperwork is dense in cleaning, thin elsewhere.** Cleaning has developed market pages; equivalent depth for gardeners, window cleaners, handymen, decorators, dog groomers, mobile beauticians is visibly thinner. That asymmetry is an opening.

## Where competitors are weak (from listings + review mining)

| Weakness | Evidence | Our counter |
|---|---|---|
| Fragile files: unlocked formula cells, breakable | PATTERN in review complaints | Engineering QA: protected sheets, input-cell styling, break-testing SOP |
| Instructions as afterthought | PATTERN | Quick-start PDF + walkthrough video in every listing, stated in title/images |
| One format sold as "works in both" | PATTERN | Native Sheets AND native Excel builds in every purchase |
| US-centric defaults sold worldwide | PATTERN | UK-first line: £, DD/MM, UK tax year, HMRC categories; country stated in title |
| Static product, no updates | Common (lifetime updates is a differentiator only the leaders use) | Free tax-year updates policy from day one |
| CRM/lead trackers with no sales method inside | Observed across the family: formatted tables, no stage logic, cadence, or pipeline maths | Products that encode actual pipeline discipline (our domain) |
| Trades kits are documents only | Cleaning kits = invoice/quote/contract PDFs; money+job tracking rarely integrated | Integrated "run the whole business" sheet per trade |
| No support promise | Rarely advertised | 24h reply promise on every listing |

## What is overpriced / underpriced

- Generic budget/lead trackers at £3-7 are underpriced commodities; competing there is a race to the bottom. AVOID.
- Accountant-credible bookkeeping at USD 25-70 off-Etsy (Paper and Spark) shows the ceiling is far above typical Etsy pricing: the on-Etsy £15-25 slot for a genuinely deep UK product is open.
- 20-35 page Canva welcome packets at £10-40 are priced on page count, not outcome; we do not fight on design.

## What is outdated

- Tax-year-branded listings that stop updating (2023 editions still live) leak trust; an always-current shop reads premium by contrast.
- Pre-MTD record-keeping products that never mention quarterly habits will age badly after April 2026; nobody visible owns the "MTD-ready records" positioning yet on Etsy. That window will not stay open long. INFERRED.

## Positioning opportunities (summary)

1. **"UK small business money, done properly"**: UK-first bookkeeping/tax line with engineering-grade QA and real support: underserved intersection of the proven category.
2. **Per-trade business-in-a-spreadsheet**: cleaning playbook exists; replicate structure across 8-10 underserved UK service trades.
3. **Sales-method-inside CRM sheets**: the only family where depth of domain knowledge (ours) is visibly absent from supply.
4. Landlord line as fast-follow (meet the leader's function, beat on support/format parity/updates), not as the spearhead.
