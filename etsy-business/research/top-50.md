## How to read this

The 100-point totals cluster into four bands:

- **80+ (ranks 1-7): the business.** UK money-admin and per-trade operating kits plus the CRM line. Proven demand signals (multi-thousand-review comparables or dense market pages), premium pricing, our differentiation is structural.
- **70-79 (ranks 8-26): the catalogue.** Verticals and calculators that clone the winners' engineering with new skins and new keywords. These are months 2-4 listings.
- **60-69 (ranks 27-38): opportunistic.** Real markets where our edge is partial (design-led niches, Notion format, HR adjacency). Enter only with a specific angle.
- **Below 60 (ranks 39-50): documented and declined.** Saturated consumer categories (scored honestly: demand 10/15, competition 1-3/10), IP minefields (SVG), and the policy-excluded prompt packs at rank 50 with policy/IP score 0.
