# Etsy policy and fees: what is actually allowed, and what it actually costs (UK seller)

All findings dated 2026-08-31. Direct fetches of etsy.com are blocked from this research environment, so policy pages were verified through multiple independent secondary sources quoting the current policies, plus Etsy pages surfaced in search results. Where sources agree the label is REPORTED (consistent); nothing below is invented. Re-confirm the fee percentages inside Seller Dashboard before launch (10 minutes of work).

---

## 1. The Creativity Standards now explicitly cover digital downloads

- Etsy's Creativity Standards (introduced 2024) were tightened on **10 June 2025**: digital downloads were explicitly added, reselling third-party templates as your own was prohibited, and enforcement on undisclosed AI listings increased. REPORTED (consistent across sources). Sources: [bulkmockup.com](https://www.bulkmockup.com/etsy-policy-changes/), [ecombalance.com](https://ecombalance.com/ai-content-policies-2026/), [promptlesspress.com](https://www.promptlesspress.com/blog-etsy-ai-policy-2026-digital-products).
- Digital items must qualify as **"Designed by a seller"**: your own original design or substantial creative input. A commercial licence to resell someone else's file does not make it "original". REPORTED.

## 2. AI rules

- **AI-assisted and AI-generated products are allowed**, with mandatory disclosure. Requirements reported consistently: categorize as Designed by a seller, set the production dropdowns honestly, and **state AI involvement in the listing description / AI disclosure option**. Listings flagged for missing disclosure can be deactivated without warning. REPORTED. Sources: [inkfluenceai.com](https://www.inkfluenceai.com/blog/etsy-ai-disclosure-explained-2026), [artomate.app](https://www.artomate.app/blog/etsy-ai-disclosure-policy-2026), [promptlesspress.com](https://www.promptlesspress.com/blog-can-you-use-ai-to-sell-on-etsy).
- **Selling collections of AI prompts is NOT allowed.** This is reported as an explicit restriction. Prompt packs are out of scope for any shop we build. REPORTED. Source: [promptlesspress.com](https://www.promptlesspress.com/blog-can-you-use-ai-to-sell-on-etsy). (Note: listings still visible in Etsy search for "chatgpt prompts" show enforcement is incomplete; building on an enforcement gap is how shops die. We do not.)
- Practical reading for our products: spreadsheets and templates where the *design, structure and formulas are our own work*, with AI used as a production tool, are the safe zone. Where AI-generated content ships inside the product (e.g. AI-written guide text), disclose it in the description.

## 3. PLR / MRR / reselling

- PLR ("private label rights") and MRR ("master resell rights") packs conflict with the originality requirement unless substantially reworked, and Etsy has not published a rework threshold. Duplicate-content detection plus member reporting makes this a suspension vector. Effective risk: **HIGH**. REPORTED. Sources: [goldcityventures.com](https://goldcityventures.com/can-you-sell-plr-on-etsy-ultimate-guide/), [theplrstore.com](https://www.theplrstore.com/what-etsys-2025-policy-changes-mean-for-plr-and-digital-sellers.html).
- Decision: **no PLR/MRR anywhere in this business.** Everything is built from scratch, which our production system makes cheap anyway.

## 4. Canva and third-party assets

- Selling Canva-based templates is allowed, but: templates containing **Canva Pro content may only be delivered as a Canva template link** (never exported as a file); free content may be exported; the design must be an original composition, not a re-skinned Canva template or standalone stock element. Standard delivery: a PDF containing the template link. REPORTED. Sources: [gelato.com](https://www.gelato.com/blog/how-to-sell-canva-templates-on-etsy), [theprintondemandplaybook.com](https://theprintondemandplaybook.com/blog/how-to-sell-canva-templates-on-etsy-legally), [shieldmyshop.com](https://www.shieldmyshop.com/blog/2026-04-12-selling-canva-templates-etsy-licensing-copyright-risks).
- Fonts/stock: only assets with explicit commercial redistribution rights; keep a licence register (see qa-sop.md). Spreadsheet products sidestep most of this: system fonts, own formulas, own layouts.

## 5. IP and trademark

- Common digital-seller takedown causes: trademarked terms in titles/tags (brand names, character names, "Disney", sports teams), copyrighted characters in SVGs/art, copied listing photos/copy. REPORTED (widely documented).
- Our exposure is low by design: business spreadsheets and document templates reference no brands. One trap to avoid: product and listing copy must not use "QuickBooks", "Xero", "FreeAgent" etc. except in truthful comparative statements, and never in tags.

## 6. Claims risk specific to our category (finance/tax products)

- A spreadsheet is not "MTD-compatible software" in the HMRC sense (quarterly submission needs recognised/bridging software). Product copy must say **"MTD-ready record keeping"** or "keeps the digital records MTD requires", never "MTD compliant filing". VERIFIED context: [gocardless.com](https://gocardless.com/blog/mtd-itsa-sole-traders-landlords-2026-guide), [companiesmadesimple.com](https://www.companiesmadesimple.com/blogs/compliance-and-legal/making-tax-digital-for-sole-traders).
- Every finance product carries a visible "records tool, not tax advice" disclaimer. This is a copy rule, not a legal opinion.

## 7. Seller standards and suspension risks

Reported common causes for digital shops: undisclosed AI, IP strikes, duplicate/PLR content, review manipulation, sudden policy-flag on new shops doing high volume fast, poor response times tanking Star Seller metrics. Mitigations: disclose, build original, answer messages under 24h, never incentivize reviews (against policy), drip listings rather than mass-uploading 100 in a day. REPORTED/ANECDOTAL mix.

## 8. UK fee stack (2026)

REPORTED, consistent across UK-specific sources: [craftbatch.co.uk](https://craftbatch.co.uk/blog/etsy-seller-fees-uk-explained/), [livingthehustle.co.uk](https://livingthehustle.co.uk/etsy-fees-uk-2026-what-you-actually-pay-and-how-to-price-around-them/), [huskai.co.uk](https://huskai.co.uk/blog/etsy-fees-explained-2026), [globalfeecalculator.com](https://globalfeecalculator.com/blog/etsy-fees-uk-explained/):

| Fee | Rate |
|---|---|
| Listing fee | £0.15 per listing / renewal (4 months or per sale) |
| Transaction fee | 6.5% of sale price |
| Payment processing (UK) | 4% + £0.20 |
| UK Regulatory Operating Fee | 0.48% (raised from 0.32% effective 22 June 2026) |
| VAT on Etsy's fees | 20% added to all fees if no VAT number on file (applies to us pre-registration) |
| Offsite Ads (only when Etsy's ad drove the sale) | 15% optional under £8,000/yr revenue; 12% mandatory above |
| One-time new shop setup fee | ~$29 / £stated at signup. REPORTED; confirm exact £ at registration |
| Share & Save | Transaction fee cut 6.5% → 2.5% on sales from your own shared links ([etsy.com seller handbook](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1187231088945), [help.etsy.com](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/16981332744087-How-to-Save-on-Etsy-Fees-with-the-Share-Save-Program)) |

### Worked take-rates (no offsite ad, VAT on fees included, renewal fee counted per sale)

| Sale price | Total fees | Take rate | Net |
|---|---|---|---|
| £4.99 | ~£1.08 | ~21.6% | £3.91 |
| £9.99 | ~£1.74 | ~17.4% | £8.25 |
| £14.99 | ~£2.40 | ~16.0% | £12.59 |
| £24.99 | ~£3.71 | ~14.9% | £21.28 |
| £34.99 | ~£5.03 | ~14.4% | £29.96 |

Two structural conclusions: (1) fixed fees punish sub-£5 products, so the catalogue's centre of gravity belongs at £10-25; (2) an offsite-ad-attributed sale costs ~34% all-in at £14.99, still profitable on a zero-marginal-cost product, so leave offsite ads on.

## 9. Policy and IP risk ratings by product type

| Product type | Policy risk | IP risk | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spreadsheets, own formulas (Excel/Sheets) | LOW | LOW | Safest category on the platform |
| Business document templates (Word/Docs/PDF, own design) | LOW | LOW | Avoid legal-document claims (contracts = MEDIUM, jurisdictional) |
| Canva templates via template link | LOW-MEDIUM | MEDIUM | Canva licence rules; Pro-content link-only |
| Notion templates | LOW | LOW | Delivery via link; smaller Etsy audience |
| Printable PDFs, own design | LOW | LOW | Saturated, but compliant |
| SVG/cut files | MEDIUM | HIGH | Character/brand infringement is endemic |
| AI-generated art printables | MEDIUM | MEDIUM | Allowed with disclosure; quality-flood category |
| Prompt packs | HIGH | LOW | Reported as prohibited. Excluded |
| PLR/MRR resale | HIGH | HIGH | Excluded |
| Products with stock assets | MEDIUM | MEDIUM | Licence register required |
| Tax/finance record tools | LOW | LOW | Copy rules: no advice claims, no "MTD compliant filing" |
