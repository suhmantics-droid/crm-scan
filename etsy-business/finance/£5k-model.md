# The £5,000/month model

Dated 2026-08-31. Same discipline: modelled, not forecast.

## The arithmetic
£5,000 gross/month at AOV £17 (bundle-heavier mix) = **~294 orders/month ≈ 9.8/day**. Net ≈ £4,250.
Traffic at 2.5% conversion: **~11,800 visits/month (~390/day)**.

## What that requires (each with its evidence state)
1. **Catalogue**: 150-250 quality listings (BASE maths: ~250 at 0.5 o/l/m gross £1,900... the gap is closed by winners and AOV, not raw count). Top digital shops are described at 50-200 optimised listings earning £8k-30k/mo (REPORTED, unaudited): the shape is precedented, not guaranteed.
2. **Winners**: 10-15 listings doing 5-25 orders/mo each (tax-season flagship, CRM, 2-3 trade kits). This is the power law working FOR us and it cannot be scheduled, only made likely (quality floor + breadth + season).
3. **AOV £17+**: bundles ≥30% of orders; premium tier (landlord multi-property, personalisation) live.
4. **Off-Etsy flywheel**: Pinterest compounding (12+ months of pins), email list (1,500+ by month 12: at 25% capture that needs ~6,000 orders lifetime... realistically 500-900 subscribers year 1: email contributes launches, not volume, in year 1), possibly Etsy Ads on proven winners at ROAS ≥3 (REPORTED viable at £15+ prices).
5. **Seasonal peaks captured twice**: Jan (self assessment) and Mar-Apr (new tax year + MTD £30k wave April 2027: 2027's spike should exceed 2026's).

## Verdict (per brief: pick one)
**POSSIBLE BUT DIFFICULT.** Not REALISTIC-by-default: the median outcome of this plan executed competently is £1-2.5k/mo, not £5k. Not UNLIKELY either: the category demonstrably supports five-figure months on catalogue scale, our production cost per listing is unusually low, and the regulatory calendar hands us two compounding Januaries. Expected timing if BASE case lands at month 6: **£5k/mo becomes plausible months 12-18**, driven by the second tax season + 200-listing catalogue + bundle AOV.

## Scenarios at month 12 (~150-200 listings)
| | LOW | BASE | HIGH |
|---|---|---|---|
| Orders/mo (Jan peak month) | 90 | 200 | 380 |
| Gross (peak month) | ~£1,350 | ~£3,400 | ~£6,460 |
| Gross (trough month) | ~£700 | ~£1,800 | ~£3,500 |

£5k+ sustained (not just January) most likely requires the Shopify layer (premium bundles £49-99 + email launches) on top of Etsy: which is exactly why shopify-strategy.md gates it on month-6 data rather than building it now.

## The two honest failure modes at this altitude
1. **Plateau at £1.5-2k**: catalogue grows but winners don't multiply. Countermove: double down on winner families only; kill breadth that isn't converting; ads on winners.
2. **Season dependence**: 60%+ of revenue in Nov-Feb. Countermove: trades/CRM lines are the counter-seasonal ballast; grow them in spring deliberately.
