# Organic acquisition strategy (zero/low budget)

Dated 2026-08-31. Channel priorities are ordered by evidence of fit for digital business tools, not by fashion. Every external link is a Share & Save link (6.5% → 2.5% transaction fee on resulting sales).

## Content rules
1. Teach the job, sell the shortcut: every piece delivers a complete useful answer; the product is the faster version.
2. UK-specific always: the differentiator is the content hook ("£ not $").
3. Batch production: one monthly 2h session drafts the month's pins/scripts (AI-drafted, human-finished); one 1h session records 8-10 shorts.
4. Accuracy gate: any content naming rates/thresholds gets the qa-sop C-pass (gov.uk check + date).
5. No income claims, no "make £5k on Etsy" meta-content: we sell tools to businesses, not dreams.

## Seasonal pushes
- Oct-Jan: self assessment content dominates (checklists, deadline countdowns, "what you can claim" series).
- Mar-Apr: new tax year + MTD wave content ("the records HMRC expects from April").
- Sept: "back to business" trades push; Nov: "get paid before Christmas" invoice-chase push.
