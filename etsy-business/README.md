# etsy-business: research and launch system

Deep-research pass (2026-08-31) answering one question: **what exact Etsy digital business should be built**, given an operator with B2B sales, CRM/GTM, procurement and process-engineering skills, near-zero startup budget, and Claude Code as a production line.

**The verdict: a UK small-business money & operations spreadsheet shop** (working name SortedSheets), spearheaded by a UK Sole Trader Bookkeeping + Self Assessment Tracker, expanding into per-trade business kits and a sales/CRM line. Full decision: `strategy/winner.md`. Full report: `FINAL-REPORT.md`.

## Map

```
research/    evidence: policy+fees, market, segments, problems, keywords (500),
             competitors, review mining, SEO framework, tools, scored top-50/20
strategy/    top-10 memos, top-3 concepts, THE WINNER, positioning, product
             architecture, catalogue strategy, Shopify graduation plan
products/    first 20 products (CSV) + 12-month roadmap
listings/    the first 10 listings: titles, 13 tags, copy, image system
operations/  AI production system, production SOP, QA SOP, customer service SOP
finance/     unit economics (CSV), £1k and £5k models, side-hustle comparison
launch/      30-day day-by-day plan, 90-day plan with kill/pivot gates, checklist
content/     organic acquisition strategy + 100 content ideas
```

## Data honesty rules used throughout

Every claim is labelled VERIFIED / REPORTED / ESTIMATED / INFERRED / ANECDOTAL / UNKNOWN. No sales figures, search volumes or revenues are invented; UNKNOWN appears where the data does not exist or needs the in-shop Marketplace Insights pull (scheduled day 2 of the 30-day plan). Environment constraint disclosed: etsy.com could not be fetched directly from this research session; listing facts come from search-surfaced snippets and are marked accordingly, with a live re-verification pass built into launch days 2-3.

## How to use this

1. Read `FINAL-REPORT.md` (the 24 answers), then `strategy/winner.md`.
2. Start `launch/30-day-plan.md` day 1.
3. Update the research files with live data as the plan's verification days produce it: this directory is the operating manual, not an archive.
