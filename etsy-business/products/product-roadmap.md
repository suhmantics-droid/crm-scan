# Product roadmap

Dated 2026-08-31. Sequencing logic: proven demand first, review velocity second, moat third, tests fourth. Engines (see strategy/product-architecture.md) are built once in weeks 1-2; everything after is skinning.

## Wave 1: launch set (days 1-30) — products 1-10 of first-20.csv
Ledger engine + Client engine built and QA'd. Ten listings live by day 30: flagship + satellites + first bundle + first two trade kits + CRM. Goal: coverage of the highest-intent UK tax keywords before the Sept-Jan season peaks, plus cheap entry SKUs (£4.99-9.99) driving first reviews.

## Wave 2: verticalization (days 31-60) — products 11-15
Trades expansion (job-sheet kit, gardener), pricing calculator, reseller test, landlord fast-follow. Kill rule: any Wave-1 listing with zero favourites and <20 views by day 45 gets one re-SEO before Wave 2 continues.

## Wave 3: packs + sales line (days 61-90) — products 16-20
Etsy-seller UK edition, market trader kit, Run Your Trade bundle, recruiter tracker, commission tracker. By day 90: ~35-45 listings including duplicate-intent variants (same product, different lead keyword + thumbnail).

## Months 4-6: depth and season
- 8-12 more trade verticals (window cleaner, mobile beautician, decorator, plumber-mate, tutor, PT) at ~1 day each.
- January push: tax-season SEO variants, price test flagship at £19.99, Etsy Ads on the two best converters only.
- Email capture live (via update-delivery flow) from month 4.

## Months 7-12: editions, premium, platform decision
- March: 2027/28 editions of every finance SKU (new listings; free updates to owners). The £30k MTD wave (Apr 2027) is the copy hook.
- Premium tier: multi-property landlord, "bookkeeper's client pack" (multi-business), personalisation add-ons.
- Shopify trigger check (strategy/shopify-strategy.md); Notion ports of the two best sellers as format tests.

## Standing rules
- 2-3 new listings/week, never batch-dumped.
- Every product passes qa-sop.md before listing; no exceptions for bundles.
- Quarterly prune per catalogue-strategy.md.
- Roadmap reorders quarterly on shop data, not on opinion.
