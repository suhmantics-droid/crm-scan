# 90-day plan

Dated 2026-08-31. Targets are BASE-case with LOW/HIGH bounds (finance/ models). Every month ends with a gate decision, not a vibe.

## Month 1 (days 1-30): launch set
Covered day-by-day in 30-day-plan.md.
**Goals**: 10 listings · first 5-10 orders (LOW 1-2 / HIGH 25) · 3+ reviews · Pinterest live (30+ pins) · real keyword volumes recorded · AOV ≥£12.
**Gate**: if 0 orders AND <300 total views by day 30 → diagnose listings (CTR? conversion?) before building more: fix discovery first.

## Month 2 (days 31-60): verticalize + iterate
- Build waves: trades kit, gardener kit, day-rate calculator, reseller tracker (the INFERRED-demand test), landlord tracker, Run Your Trade bundle. ~22-28 listings total (incl. duplicate-intent variants of the flagship for different lead keywords).
- Iterate: 14-day A/B reads on re-SEO'd listings; first price test (£16.99 vs £19.99 flagship variant).
- Content: 3 pins/day scheduled weekly; 2 shorts/week (screen demos + UK tax facts); all links Share & Save.
- Ops: email-capture flow drafted (update-delivery page) ready for month 4.
**Goals**: 25-30 listings · 15-30 orders/mo run-rate (LOW 5 / HIGH 60) · 10+ reviews · 1 listing showing winner behaviour (≥20 favourites or ≥5 orders).
**Gate**: reseller tracker gets 45 days of data; kill or double based on views/favourites, not hope.

## Month 3 (days 61-90): season ramp
- Build: Etsy-seller UK edition, market-trader kit, recruiter tracker, commission tracker, remaining bundle SKUs; tax-season SEO variants of the finance line ("self assessment 2025 26", "tax return spreadsheet") targeting the autumn ramp. ~35-45 listings.
- First Etsy Ads test ONLY if a listing has proven conversion ≥2.5%: £2/day on that listing, ROAS ≥3 to continue (never on sub-£10 SKUs).
- Review flywheel check: follow-up flow producing reviews at ~15-20% of orders?
**Goals**: 35-45 listings · 40-80 orders/mo (LOW 15 / HIGH 140) · £400-800/mo gross (LOW £150 / HIGH £1,600) · 25+ reviews · conversion ≥2% shop-wide · AOV ≥£14.
**Gate (the big one, day 90)**:
- BASE or better → commit to months 4-6 plan (trade verticals ×8, January push, email capture live).
- Between LOW and BASE → one focused month on the top-2 performing families only; no new families.
- LOW or worse across ALL of orders, views AND reviews → invoke the portfolio decision in finance/side-hustle-comparison.md: halve Etsy hours, redirect to the crm-scan cash engine, keep Etsy compounding passively. Written now so future-us can't rationalize past it.

## Metrics tracked weekly (one row per week in the ops sheet)
Listings live · views · visits · favourites · orders · conversion · revenue · AOV · reviews (count/avg) · top-3 listings by orders · Pinterest outbound clicks · email signups (from month 4).
